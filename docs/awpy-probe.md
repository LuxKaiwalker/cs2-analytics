# Resolved decisions

Everything below was **observed** on `valve-de_mirage-472514809.dem`, not assumed. These are the
facts the normalized model and analytics are built on.

| # | Question | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does `kills` expose a flash-assist flag? | **Yes — `assist_flash` (Boolean)**, not `assistedflash`. 20 rows have an assister, 5 of them flash. | The kill-assist / flash-assist split is read directly from the parser. No blind-matching heuristic needed. |
| 2 | Are `round_start` / `round_end` events present? | **No.** Also absent: `item_purchase`. `player_blind`, `flashbang_detonate`, `player_team` are present. | Never depend on raw round events. awpy reconstructs `rounds` and `item_events` from game-rules / inventory state — this is the main reason awpy 3 was chosen. |
| 3 | What does `snapshots(ticks=[freeze_end…])` return? | 13 ticks → **130 rows, exactly 10 players per tick**, with `side` and `health` (all 100 at freeze end), in 0.12 s. | Confirmed as the primary roster signal: per-round side *and* `present_at_freeze`, without parsing the full tick stream. `roster_source="snapshots"`. |
| 4 | Is `blinds` populated on a Valve MM demo? | **Yes, 17 rows** — including team-flashes (terrorist flashing terrorist). | Flash metrics are available here, but `attacker_side != victim_side` filtering is mandatory. Other demos may still yield an empty frame, so the availability path stays. |
| 5 | Utility damage weapon strings | `damages.weapon` ∈ {`''`, ak47, awp, deagle, glock, **hegrenade**, hkp2000, **inferno**, knife, m4a1, **molotov**, mp9, ssg08, taser}. Both `inferno` *and* `molotov` appear. | Utility set = {hegrenade, inferno, molotov, incgrenade, firebomb} as a superset; unknown weapon strings raise a warning, never a failure. |
| 6 | World / self damage and kills | One damage row has `attacker=null, weapon=''` (fall damage). Five kills have `weapon='world'` **with attacker == victim** (post-match suicides at ticks 40206–41067). | `valid_damage` requires an attacker. **`is_self_kill` must be tested before `is_team_kill`** — otherwise self-kills are miscounted as team kills (all 5 "same side" kill rows here are self-kills; there are no genuine team kills). |
| 7 | Does damage occur after `end_tick`? | **Yes — 8 of 208 in-round damage rows** (~4%). The 5 world kills land after the *last* round's `official_end_tick`. | Vindicates partitioning the tick timeline instead of using `[start_tick, end_tick]` intervals, which would silently drop them from ADR. Post-round rows are kept but flagged `is_post_round`. |
| 8 | Can raw `dmg_health` exceed remaining health? | **Yes, up to 199** (deagle), with `health_post = 0` and `health_pre` always the capped estimate 100. | Sequential capping at the previous `health_post` is essential; summing raw `dmg_health` would inflate ADR substantially. |
| 9 | Are `kills` / `damages` tick-ordered? | Yes (`is_sorted() == True`), **but 2 same-tick kill groups and 25 same-tick damage groups exist**. | `src_idx` (source row index) is still required as the tiebreaker for ADR capping and trade detection. |
| 10 | Do `fires` / `smokes` carry a thrower? | **No — `thrower_name` / `thrower_steamid` are null** on this demo. | Utility *throws* are derived from `grenades` (which does carry a thrower); fires/smokes attribution is marked unavailable rather than guessed. |
| 11 | `grenades` shape | 6682 trajectory rows → **63 distinct projectiles** by `(thrower_steamid, type, entity_id, entity_serial)`. Types: flashbang, he, molotov, smoke. | `flashes_thrown` must count distinct projectile groups, not rows. |
| 12 | Who is in `players`? | 11 rows: 10 real players + **`DemoRecorder` with `steamid = 0` and `side = null`** (the SourceTV recorder). All `team_clan_name` null (matchmaking). | Build the player list from the **roster** (snapshots), not from `demo.players` — the recorder never appears in snapshots, while genuine bots (also steamid 0) do. Team names fall back to "Team A"/"Team B". |
| 13 | Halftime swap coverage | **The fixture does contain a swap.** `mp_maxrounds=24` (MR12): rounds 1–12 won by T, then every player flips side at round 13, won by CT → 13–0. `round_economy` classifies round 13 as `pistol`. | Contrary to the initial read of "13–0 = one half", the real demo *does* exercise side-swap and side→team mapping. Overtime and knife rounds remain synthetic-only. |
| 14 | Memory and time | Decoding **every** dataset: **2.2 s**, peak RSS **198 MB for an 84 MB demo (2.3×)**. | `MAX_DEMO_BYTES = 400 MiB` → ~0.9 GB expected RSS, comfortable against the ~3.5 GB available. `WORKER_MEM_LIMIT_MB = 3072`. |
